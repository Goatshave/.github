# Goatshave

**Follow the problem. Build something useful.**

Goatshave is a nonprofit developer crew exploring problems we encounter in software. We build tools, improve open source, and share what we learn across technologies.

## What we'd like to work on together

- **Useful tools** — Build what we need and refine it through real use.
- **Better open source** — Improve code, documentation, tests, and design in existing projects.
- **Deeper understanding** — Reproduce problems, explore alternatives, and share the reasoning.

## Building together

We welcome curious people at any experience level. We make small changes, test what we build, and share our reasoning through constructive reviews.

## Join the conversation

Bring a question, an idea, or something you'd like to improve. Public contributions are open without crew membership.

[Share a problem or idea](https://github.com/Goatshave/.github/issues/new?template=idea.yml) · [Talk about joining](https://github.com/Goatshave/.github/issues/new?template=crew.yml) · [Contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

Issue links are public; leave out personal data and vulnerability details.
