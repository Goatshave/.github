# Working together: operations draft

**For discussion. These proposals have not been adopted.**
Agree the details and consenting owners before applying them. This document does not authorize access or spending.

## Proposed approach

- **Time:** work at your own pace and share changes to what you can take on. Avoid minimum hours or on-call expectations.
- **Ownership and access:** agree project responsibilities, review routes, release and security ownership, and the minimum permissions needed.
- **Changes and review:** discuss larger changes first. Keep reviews constructive and record reproducible checks.
- **Breaks and departure:** respect time away. Where practical, hand over open work, release state, and access responsibilities without exposing secrets.
- **Costs and support:** agree the purpose, amount, and payer before spending. Publish income, expenses, and use of funds with personal details removed.
- **Conduct and security:** keep project policies in place. Agree organization-wide confidential reporting and conflict-of-interest alternatives separately.

## Decisions to record

Confirm owners and backups, decision and access procedures, reporting contacts, and the finance publication process.
For each agreement, record the discussion, owner consent, effective date, and review date.

Adoption and owners remain undecided. No sponsorship channel or legal incorporation is claimed.
